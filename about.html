Who we are

Built to turn complex ideas into operating projects.

CJL Consulting combines project management, EPCM, commercial structuring and operating discipline to establish environmentally responsible ventures.

One accountable project-management partner from first concept to commissioned operation.

CJL Consulting is a Mauritius-based project-management company offering clients, business partners and The Green Group complete EPCM services and project-management expertise.

The company supports project finance, complex delivery, turnkey establishment and business operations. Its role is to connect strategy with the specialist technical and operating capabilities required for each opportunity.

Project managementCoordinated scope, programme, controls and stakeholder delivery.
EPCMEngineering, procurement and construction management through commissioning.
Commercial disciplineFinance strategy, due diligence and operating readiness.
