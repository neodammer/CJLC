II — Who we are

Purpose-built for the green economy.

CJL Consulting is a Mauritius-based project management company offering clients, business partners and The Green Group complete EPCM services and project-management expertise.

Our work includes raising project finance, managing complex delivery, establishing turnkey ventures and strengthening business operations. Each project is designed around three principles:

Innovative21st-century technologies applied with practical discipline.

III — The Green Group

Six divisions.
One vision.

The Green Group is structured to cover the full project lifecycle—from funding and power to agriculture, water, coal reclamation and natural medicine.

01

Technology-led project-finance mechanisms intended to support establishment of Green Group ventures.

02

Green Agriculture

Bamboo, coconut, hydroponics and aquaponics—integrated systems that produce value and restore land.

Green Water
