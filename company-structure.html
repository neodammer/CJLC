Responsibilities

Defined roles across the delivery model.

The structure is designed to keep project accountability clear while allowing more than one division to contribute to an integrated venture.

CJL ConsultingLeads project management, EPCM coordination, finance strategy, governance, commercial structuring and stakeholder delivery.
The Green GroupProvides the coordinated platform through which specialist divisions contribute sector capability and operating input.
Specialist divisionsDefine and support the relevant technical, operating and market workstreams for each project.
Project companies and partnersMay be established or engaged according to the requirements, approvals, contracts and funding structure of each opportunity.

Working together

Integration without losing specialist focus.

Combined projects can draw on several divisions while CJLC maintains a single programme, governance framework and route to decision.
